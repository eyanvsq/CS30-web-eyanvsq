How Decision Styles Differed

Millennials (Ages 28–44)

  • Focused on careful visual inspection.
  • Checked lighting, image sharpness, and textures.
  • Scored higher in video detection tests (63.3%).

SHS Students (Ages 18–19)

  • Relied mainly on quick instincts and immediate impressions.
  • Scored high on simple single-image tests (70.9%).
  • Found multiple-choice identification more challenging.
