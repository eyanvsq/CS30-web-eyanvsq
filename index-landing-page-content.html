Can You Spot Fake AI Media?

In a world where AI-generated content is becoming increasingly prevalent, the ability to distinguish between real and fake media has become a critical skill. Are you up for this challenge?

Key Study Findings

59.1% - Millennials

Higher accuracy due to taking more time to analyze visual details.

55.5% - SHS Students

Higher daily tech exposure, but relied more on initial instinct.

124 Participants

95 SHS Students and 29 Millennials surveyed in Quezon City.

How Decision Styles Differed

Millennials (Ages 28–44)

  • Focused on careful visual inspection.
  • Checked lighting, image sharpness, and textures.
  • Scored higher in video detection tests (63.3%).

SHS Students (Ages 18–19)

  • Relied mainly on quick instincts and immediate impressions.
  • Scored high on simple single-image tests (70.9%).
  • Found multiple-choice identification more challenging.

Top Visual Clues Used to Spot AI

  • Unnatural Lighting
  • Facial Distortions
  • Irregular Fingers & Hands
  • Smooth/Plastic Textures
  • Overly Complex Backgrounds
